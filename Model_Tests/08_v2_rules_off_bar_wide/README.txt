v2 -- both shots, three rules off, bar wide
===========================================

Every point on the disc gets a score for how scratch-like it is, in BOTH
photographs. The two are lined up and the LOWER of the two scores is kept, so a
mark survives only if both shots saw it -- a lamp's reflection moves when the
disc is tilted and is cut down to nothing. Only then is a threshold applied.

The three anti-reflection rules are switched off and the threshold is lowered,
because the intersection above already removes what they were guarding against.

Alignment comes from the response maps themselves rather than the centre label:
it worked on 52 of the 55 sides, and on the sides where both could be checked it
lands within a degree of an exhaustive search, where the label refused outright
on one side and was 5.5 degrees out on another.

Measured over 30 calibration records, 55 sides, 318 scratches drawn in pen:

    marks painted            37.9 a photo   (the server paints 14.8)
    recall                   58.2%          (the server, 46.5%)
    precision                85.0%          (the server, 86.6%)
    marks on a clean record  28.0           (the server, 28.0 -- unchanged)
    marks nobody has judged  68%

The folders are the same 30 records three times over: the photograph, what the
server marks on it today, and what this model marks. The painting is the
server's own -- same colour, same halo, same clip at the rim -- so what is shown
here is what the app would show.
